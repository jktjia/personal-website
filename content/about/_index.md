I'm Jamie Kai Tjia, a Master’s student in Computer Science at Northeastern University. Throughout my academic journey so far, I’ve enjoyed developing my software engineering skills, teaching as a TA for core CS classes, and discovering applications of predictive modeling to problems relevant to health and biology.

I am always looking for opportunities to collaborate with others and learn about the world at the intersection of software engineering, biology at the molecular level, and disease at the person- and public health-levels. I’m a developer with expertise in crafting efficient, well-documented, elegant code.

Trained in close reading and careful writing, as well as in deriving elegant math-based solutions from first principles, I enjoy a good book, a novel puzzle, or a mind-bending challenge. In my spare time, I enjoy playing a good board game and watching _Star Trek: Deep Space Nine_ with my cats.
